About us

St. Gentleman is a local bakery brand born in Morio with part of its roots in S. City. 

Established in 1990, we are known for the best Katsu Sandwich in Morio with the widest selection other bakes of flavours and stunning designs. We also make the most authentic pandan chiffon cakes one can find outside of Singapore. Over the years we have been expanding our product offerings to cater to a wider audience because we believe everyone deserves something sweet.

We’re passionate bakers with a commitment to our community and we plan on continuing this tradition for the years to come. We strive for excellence through innovation and creativity. We welcome you to come visit us and give your taste buds a treat at St. Gentleman.

Enjoy life, it’s delicious!
